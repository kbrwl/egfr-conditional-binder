## Appendix A — the pair table, per submitted design

For every submitted design, which binder residue faces which target
residue, and what each pair does at the two pH values the assay uses.
Generated by `analysis/22_pair_appendix.py` from
`data/derived/10-candidate-pairs.csv`, so it cannot drift from what was
measured.

Histidine is the only amino acid whose charge changes across this
range: neutral at pH 7.4, positive at pH 6.5. Aspartate and glutamate
carry a negative charge at both. A pair is correct when it is built so
that attraction appears as the pH falls, which happens in two ways —
a binder histidine facing a target acidic residue, or a binder acidic
residue facing a target histidine. Both are listed in full below.

Contacts are counted at a 4.5 Å heavy-atom cutoff and include every
residue-residue contact with the target face, charged or not. Neutral
contacts are summarised as a count rather than listed one by one.

**Every distance here is measured on a predicted structure, and every
pH statement is an inference from which residues face each other rather
than a measurement of binding.**
